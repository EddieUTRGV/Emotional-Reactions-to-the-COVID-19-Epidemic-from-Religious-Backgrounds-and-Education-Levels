# Emotional Reactions to the COVID-19 Epidemic from Religious Backgrounds and Education Levels

Do Spiritual Practicioners have an association to the frequency of people who perceived the COVID-19 epidemic as a legitimate issue.

Were these religious people more or less likely to adhere to global regulations like wearing masks or keeping the minimum distance?

The research conducted has collected data for categorigal variables, most based on the emotional responses many had during the beginning of the pandemic. The study was focused on a sample based in 4 European countries in April 2020.



My explanatory variables topic is about a persons religious standing. My datasets variable name for it is A3.8 and is categorical.

My response variable is about wether or not a person believed COVID-19 was exaggerated. My datasets variable name for it is A5.3 and is categorical.

Null Hypothesis: There is no relationship between the frequency in which people believed COVID-19 was not a real issue and religious orientation.

Alternative Hypothesis: There is there is a relationship between the frequency in which people believed COVID-19 to not be a serious issue and religious orientation.

Since both my variables are categorigal, a chi-square test would be the most appropriate to asses the relationship.
