# Emotional Reactions to the COVID-19 Epidemic from Religious Backgrounds and Education Levels

My project will be based off of a study conducted by David Abadi, Irene Arnaldo, and Agneta Fischer, "A Dataset of Social-Psychological and Emotional Reactions During the COVID-19 Pandemic Across Four European Countries."
This study on the Journal of Open Psychology Data.

I am interested in seeing how different demographics, based on their religious orientation and their level of education, percieved the COVID-19 epidemic as a legitimate issue.

How seriously did people within a religious or spiritual orientation take COVID-19? What was their education level?

Did these demographics adhere to the government regulations?
